# Website Launch
## Everything in One Place

Welcome to my new website!  
I intend to use this space as a central hub for my personal projects and creative work — a place where everything I’m doing can live together.

## Why This Site Exists
Over time, I’ve ended up with projects scattered across different platforms and folders. This website is my attempt to bring all of that into one place, where it’s easier to organize, revisit, and share.

Some sections will be more polished than others, and some things will remain very much *work in progress* — that’s intentional.

## The Blog
Alongside my projects, I’ll also be maintaining this blog. I plan to post regular updates on what I’ve been up to, whether that’s:

- Progress on personal or academic projects  
- Development updates on my game *CRISPR*  
- Reflections on trips (like my time in Greece)  
- Thoughts on holidays, creative work, or anything else I feel like writing about  

There’s no strict theme — this is meant to be flexible and personal.

## Looking Ahead
This site will continue to change as I experiment, iterate, and add new content. If you’re reading this early on, you’re seeing it at the very beginning.

Thanks for stopping by — more soon.
